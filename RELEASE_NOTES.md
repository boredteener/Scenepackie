Scenepackie 0.4 keeps things simple: choose a video, pick a face, review the clips, and export.

### What's changed
- Finished character scans and matching clips are saved locally, so reopening the same unchanged movie doesn't mean starting over.
- Clip selections are remembered, including the clips you've unchecked.
- **Clear saved scans** removes saved scanning data without deleting source videos or exports.
- Portable Mac builds join Windows: Apple Silicon and Intel downloads are separate.

### Still here
Clip previews with sound, individual MP4s, a continuous full scenepack MP4, optional ZIP exports, and the same cream-and-brown interface.

### Downloads
Pick the ZIP that matches your machine. Extract it and open the app. No Python, Homebrew, or separate FFmpeg installation needed. Because these builds aren't code-signed or notarized, your system may ask you to approve the app before first launch.

Scenepackie works locally and doesn't upload footage. Character matching isn't perfect: check each scenepack before publishing.
